# DOL Weekly UI-Claims Labor-Shock Training Design

## Scope and competing approaches

This is a versionless, training-only feasibility screen on the fixed 527 symbols appearing in the frozen 2021-2023 event cube: a **coverage-limited sample, not the full market**. The question is whether newly published unemployment-insurance claims create a one-session relative-stock effect through independently estimated broad-equity sensitivity. This is a labor-separation/continuing-unemployment information source, not a retry of the failed SEC issuer filings, news, FINRA, Cboe, Fed policy, CFTC positioning, USPTO patents, or EIA petroleum inventory.

The source assessment compared three free official directions. Monthly Census retail data have only roughly 36 training releases and are too sparse for a 40-session retained-cell floor. Treasury auction outcomes have more instruments but require a defensible auction-to-equity-duration contract before a comparable 527-symbol screen. DOL weekly claims have 156 release-specific official archive links in the three training years and two explicit national first-release measures, so they are the selected next line. This is an economic hypothesis, not an established edge.

## Immutable source and first-publication semantics

The only training source is the [U.S. Department of Labor Office of Unemployment Insurance archive form](https://oui.doleta.gov/unemploy/claims_arch.asp), selecting `report=press` separately for 2021, 2022, and 2023, and then each exact release-specific official PDF URL linked in those three returned HTML year pages. The pre-registration source audit counted exactly 52 links per year. Before any PDF download, freeze the three raw annual HTML responses and the ordered date/URL inventory, request method/body, HTTP response metadata, retrieval timestamp, byte counts and SHA-256. Then retrieve only those links sequentially at no more than one request per second, preserving every raw PDF and its metadata/hash. No guessed filename, current mutable `https://www.dol.gov/ui/data.pdf`, revised downloadable time series, mirror, login or API key may substitute. Dated official archive links are not proof that historical bytes are legally immutable: any changed hash on re-fetch, duplicate/missing link, redirect outside the official archive, or incomplete year fails closed.

From each PDF, extract **only the first page** after mechanical whitespace normalization: the explicit embargo/publication date and the first-publication `advance figure for seasonally adjusted initial claims was N` and `advance number for seasonally adjusted insured unemployment ... was N` values. The exact top-page phrases are illustrated by a [2020 pretraining DOL PDF](https://oui.doleta.gov/press/2020/010220.pdf); no 2021-2023 PDF body was used to define the extraction. Accept comma-formatted nonnegative integers; require exactly one unambiguous match per field and an explicit Eastern 8:30 a.m. release statement. Compare the publication date to the annual HTML listing and date-bearing PDF URL. Preserve raw extraction text hashes and all failures. Never take later historical-chart rows, a following week's revision, or a current database value as the previous release's initial value.

The archive says normal publication is Thursday 8:30 a.m. Eastern, with holiday exceptions. The first usable session is the first frozen sample trading session **strictly after** the PDF's explicit publication date, and the signal expires after that one session. This conservative rule does not depend on the exact publication hour and excludes same-day market reaction from the tested outcome.

## Frozen labor state, exposure and five families

For each of the two first-release measures, compute `innovation = current first-published value - median(the strictly prior 12 complete first-published release values)`. Earlier releases remain source inventory but cannot activate a family. These are deviations from prior releases, **not analyst-consensus surprises**. The five frozen release-state predicates are: (1) initial-claims deterioration (`innovation > 0`), (2) initial-claims improvement (`innovation < 0`), (3) insured-unemployment deterioration (`innovation > 0`), (4) insured-unemployment improvement (`innovation < 0`), and (5) concordant deterioration (both innovations `> 0`). Zero innovation is inactive; no magnitude threshold, percentile optimization, alternate seasonal series, pandemic carve-out or extra family is allowed.

SPY, present in the fixed sample, is the sole cross-stock reference and is excluded from candidate positions. For each publication, estimate each other symbol's ordinary least-squares beta to SPY from exactly the most recent 60 **paired, finite, distinct `bar_idx=5` opening-return proxy sessions strictly before the publication date**. The cube's `session_return` differs across bars; bar 5 is a roughly 25-minute opening proxy, not full-day performance. Requiring a cutoff before publication also excludes the announcement-day bar-5 reaction from the exposure estimate. Duplicate keys are invalid, missing sessions are not zeros, and zero SPY variance or nonfinite beta excludes that symbol for the release. No beta sign filter is applied: improvements rank eligible symbols by descending beta, while deterioration and concordant deterioration rank by ascending beta; symbol ascending breaks exact ties. This is a long-only cross-sectional sensitivity hypothesis, not an issuer-sector or employer-identity inference. Only the top K available, uniquely keyed symbols may enter an equal-weight, no-overnight position.

## Coverage gate before post-availability outcomes

Require all 156 indexed PDFs: 52 unique, valid, first-page-parsed releases in each of 2021, 2022 and 2023; all index/PDF/extraction hashes consistent, no missing or duplicate release. Require at least 200 distinct non-SPY symbols with a valid 60-pair beta somewhere in training, at least 150 valid eligible symbols on at least 100 distinct release-availability sessions, and at least 20,000 symbol-release exposure pairs. Require each of the five predicates to activate on at least 30 releases overall and at least five in each training year after the 12-release warm-up. Record separately source, PDF schema, calendar, paired-history, duplicate-key, variance and outcome-unavailability losses. A failed source or coverage threshold freezes `ABANDON_DOL_UI_CLAIMS_COVERAGE_GATE`, `cells_completed=0`, and no post-availability return load; do not modify the map, families or floors.

A read-only pre-registration structural audit of the frozen cube (bar-5 presence only; no DOL training PDF or outcome ranking) found 116/152 Friday proxy sessions with at least 150 non-SPY symbols having 60 SPY-paired observations strictly before the preceding Thursday. Actual release dates, finite betas and family coverage remain unknown, so this is not a coverage pass.

## Frozen 400-cell diagnostic and terminal rule

Only if coverage passes, evaluate five families × decision bars `(2,5,11,17,23)` × holding bars `(1,2,4,6)` × top counts `(1,3,5,10)` = exactly 400 cells, with 9 bp standard cost, 18 bp stress cost, and a one-bar delayed entry at 9 bp. A genuine zero-signal sample session is flat; a missing selected outcome is explicitly invalid and **never silently zero-filled, treated as cash, or replaced by another stock**. A retained cell needs at least 40 independent observed signal sessions and at least 10 in each training year, full-calendar annualized 9 bp net return at least 20%, IR at least 0.8, maximum drawdown below 20%, at least two positive calendar years, and positive annualized returns under both 18 bp and delayed-entry tests. Require retained cells in at least two distinct families. A training pass yields only `ACQUIRE_DEVELOPMENT_DOL_UI_CLAIMS_DATA`, a proposal to obtain development data; otherwise `ABANDON_DOL_UI_CLAIMS_NO_VERSION_CREATED`. No development or consumed period is loaded for ranking. No strategy admission, broker, submit/cancel, Paper activation, observation-pool change, order route or shutdown is authorized.
