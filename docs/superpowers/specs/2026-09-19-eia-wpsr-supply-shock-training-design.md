# EIA WPSR Physical-Supply Shock Training Design

## Scope and causal hypothesis

This is a versionless training-feasibility screen of a physically distinct return source: the U.S. Energy Information Administration (EIA) Weekly Petroleum Status Report (WPSR) inventory release. Unexpectedly large inventory draws may benefit stocks with an independently estimated, strictly historical positive energy-market exposure. The fixed input is the 527 symbols present in the frozen event cube during 2021-2023, a **coverage-limited sample, not the full market**. XLE is the sole energy-return reference and is excluded from candidate positions; no issuer-name or sector guess is used. No later-period ranking, strategy admission, Paper activation, pool mutation, or routing follows from a training pass.

This is not a retry of the CFTC positioning or Fed macro families: the information is EIA's newly released physical barrels-in-storage, rather than investor positions, volatility, monetary policy, or issuer disclosures. It is still only a hypothesis, not a known edge.

## Official source and point-in-time contract

The sole source is EIA's [WPSR previous-issues archive](https://www.eia.gov/petroleum/supply/weekly/archive/). Each 2021-2023 dated issue page states its release date and links its own Table 4 CSV. For example, [January 6, 2021](https://www.eia.gov/petroleum/supply/weekly/archive/2021/2021_01_06/wpsr_2021_01_06.php) and [January 5, 2023](https://www.eia.gov/petroleum/supply/weekly/archive/2023/2023_01_05/wpsr_2023_01_05.php) retain release-specific Table 4 links. EIA's [schedule](https://www.eia.gov/petroleum/supply/weekly/schedule.php) says Tables 1-14 CSV are normally released after 10:30 a.m. Eastern on Wednesday, with holiday exceptions. To avoid relying on the hour or a server Last-Modified timestamp, the release is usable only on the first frozen sample trading session strictly **after** the page's stated release date and expires after that session. Dated archived files are source versions, not a guarantee of legal immutability; preserve the archive-index HTML, each dated issue HTML, each Table 4 CSV, URL, retrieval time, HTTP status, byte count, ETag/Last-Modified if present, and local SHA-256. A changed file hash on re-fetch fails closed.

Before downloading any 2021-2023 Table 4 CSV, parse the official archive index into a complete ordered list of 2021-2023 release-page URLs and each page's exact linked Table 4 CSV URL. Freeze that list and the raw index/page hashes. Do not synthesize URLs, use EIA's live revised time-series API, substitute mirrors, or patch missing issues. Respect EIA automated retrieval policy with at most one request per second. HTTP failures remain explicit and retriable; an incomplete three-year set fails coverage.

The 2020 pre-training Table 4 schema probe showed exact row labels `Commercial (Excluding SPR)`, `Total Motor Gasoline`, `Distillate Fuel Oil`, and `Total Stocks (Excluding SPR)` and a `Difference` field. The 2021-2023 files must independently pass those exact labels and an unambiguous current-week `Difference` column; reject schema drift rather than guessing. Parse comma-formatted numeric strings mechanically, preserve original CSV bytes and all parse errors. `Difference` is current stocks minus previous-week stocks, in million barrels. No consensus forecast is available, so the indicator is an **inventory-change innovation versus prior releases**, not an analyst surprise.

## Frozen exposure and families

Use only the event cube's 2021-2023 `session_return` at the frozen `bar_idx=5` as a **25-minute opening-return proxy**, not as a full-day return. The event cube carries different `session_return` values at different decision bars, so mixing bars would be invalid. Require a unique `(symbol, session_date, bar_idx=5)` row; otherwise mark that key invalid. XLE is present in the frozen sample and is the only reference return. At each release-availability session, compute each other symbol's ordinary least-squares beta to XLE from exactly the most recent 60 **paired observed bar-5 sessions strictly before availability**. Require 60 distinct pairs, finite values, and positive XLE variance. Missing event sessions are not zero returns or cash. A beta is eligible only if positive. Rank all causally eligible sample symbols together by descending beta, then symbol ascending for ties; equal-weight long-only top K, no overnight holdings. XLE itself is never a candidate. This exposure is an opening co-movement proxy, not an issuer-industry classification or a full-day commodity beta.

For each of the four exact Table 4 rows, let `innovation = median(the strictly prior 12 complete release Differences for that row) - current Difference`. Require 12 strictly prior releases with valid values; a family fires only when innovation is positive. The five frozen families are: commercial crude draw innovation, motor gasoline draw innovation, distillate draw innovation, total stocks excluding SPR draw innovation, and concordant draw innovation (at least two of crude/gasoline/distillate positive on the same release). The fifth family's ranking is the same beta ranking; it is a separate release-state predicate. No build/short variant, magnitude threshold, row substitution, sector guess, or parameter search is allowed. For an active release, all five decision bars see the same already-public signal on the next session.

## Coverage gate before outcomes

Before loading post-availability returns, require all raw index/pages/CSV hashes consistent and every official 2021-2023 release page to have one Table 4 file, with at least 48 distinct valid releases in each year and at least 150 total. Require at least 150 unique non-XLE sample symbols with valid prior-60-session beta on at least 100 separate availability sessions, and at least 20,000 valid symbol-release pairs overall. Require at least 40 active releases for each of the five families and at least 10 active releases per family in each of 2021, 2022, and 2023. Record source, schema, pairing, nonpositive-beta, and missing-session losses separately. Any threshold or hash failure emits `ABANDON_EIA_WPSR_COVERAGE_GATE` with zero return cells run; no coverage-based tuning. A pre-registration-only structural audit of the frozen cube found 279 non-XLE symbols with at least 60 paired bar-5/XLE sessions over the entire training interval and, on a simple Thursday proxy calendar, 107 sessions with at least 150 such symbols; no EIA training CSV or post-release outcome was inspected to set this gate.

## Frozen diagnostic and retention

Only after coverage passes, evaluate exactly 400 cells: five families × decision bars `(2,5,11,17,23)` × holding bars `(1,2,4,6)` × top counts `(1,3,5,10)`. Use the existing frozen intraday evaluator, 9 bp standard cost, 18 bp stress cost, and one-bar delayed entry at 9 bp. Zero-signal sessions are valid observed flat sessions; missing outcomes are not filled with zero. A retained cell needs at least 40 independent signal sessions and 10 in each training year; full-calendar annualized net return at least 20%, IR at least 0.8, maximum drawdown below 20%, at least two positive calendar years, positive 18 bp annualized return, and positive delayed annualized return. Proceed only if at least two distinct families retain cells. A pass means `ACQUIRE_DEVELOPMENT_EIA_WPSR_DATA` as a data-acquisition suggestion, never strategy admission. Otherwise emit `ABANDON_EIA_WPSR_NO_VERSION_CREATED`.

No development or consumed period is loaded for ranking. No broker, submit/cancel, Paper activation, observation-pool change, order route, or host shutdown is authorized. Publish tests, immutable external data hashes, tracked terminal evidence, and tagged local fallback memory; push each completed stage.
