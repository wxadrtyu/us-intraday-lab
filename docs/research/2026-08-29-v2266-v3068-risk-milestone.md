## Frozen candidate

The frozen v1254 entry factors, selected symbols and dynamic anchor/component weights remain
unchanged. Only the anchor sleeve receives the new post-entry policy:

- a hard exit requires both a 4% selected-ETF loss and SPY down at least 0.25% from the sleeve
  entry;
- profit protection activates after a completed selected-ETF close reaches 1.5% above entry and
  exits after a 2.5% peak-to-close giveback;
- every exit is confirmed by a complete five-minute close and executes at the next five-minute
  open;
- after an exit, one re-entry is permitted only after the selected ETF closes at least 0.25%
  above the exit open and SPY has recovered to no worse than 0.25% below the original entry;
- re-entry executes at the next five-minute open and charges a second full scenario round-trip
  cost;
- if both frozen sleeves select the same leveraged ETF, total gross is capped at 0.775; otherwise
  gross remains at most one;
- the strategy remains long-only and flat overnight.

## Native nulls and remaining failures

The v3067 marginal-profit null compared one re-entry against the identical exit/cap policy with
no re-entry across all 43 pre-null survivors. The frozen v2968 observed marginal compounded
improvement was 1.0265, below the session-permutation and safe-circular-shift maxT 95% thresholds
of 1.1883 and 1.1823. This rejects the claim that re-entry supplies independently timed alpha.

The v3068 null tests the purpose for which the overlay was designed: timing-specific MDD and
expected-shortfall reduction relative to matched-validity v1254. Its statistic is the weakest,
across all three scenarios, of MDD reduction divided by 20% and tail reduction divided by 15%.
The observed statistic was 1.2643. Across 200 repetitions and all 43 survivors, the family-wise
95% maxT thresholds were -0.5600 for session permutation and -0.6953 for safe circular shift.
The risk-timing null therefore passes. Evidence SHA-256 is
`a70b53ea333120bc1a40456cab940c2471751bc73dd56eb9da7ead3936213ff6`.

The cumulative 112,105-cell Bonferroni test remains failed, as does the inherited v45 factory
null. These failures are not relabeled or waived. The milestone classification means the new
risk timing is empirically non-random under the preregistered family-wise test; it does not mean
independent alpha, real-money readiness or automatic Paper admission.

strategy was modified or deleted. A later Paper observation requires explicit authorization and

new sessions without excessive extra exits/re-entries, not whether already consumed 2026 can be
