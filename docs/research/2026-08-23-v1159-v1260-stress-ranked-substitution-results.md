transfer; all 300 failed cumulative Bonferroni.

## Comparison and decision

Relative to v1102, v1254 improves standard OOS annualized return by 2.61 percentage points, 18 bp
by 2.23 points, delay by 1.83 points, MDD by 0.30 points and IR by 0.044. Consumed 2026 Q1 is
essentially unchanged. The costs are 2.31 points less consumed-2026 total return and a much thinner
historical return margin.

Relative to v449, v1254 improves standard OOS by 3.51 points, 18 bp by 2.78 points, delay by 2.60
points, MDD by about 0.23 points and IR by 0.034. Consumed-2026 total is about 3.00 points lower and
historical transfer is weaker.

The historical result remains positive and below the 20% MDD gate, but its narrow margin is a clear
warning rather than evidence of independent transfer. The inherited v45 factory-null failure and
global Bonferroni failure remain permanent labels. Paper admission requires separate user
authorization.
