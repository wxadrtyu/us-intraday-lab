## Conclusion

The tested overnight-dislocation family does not provide a stable return source.
The apparent result is concentrated in one development fold and in post-Q1
2026; using it would amount to selecting a regime-specific outcome. The next
campaign changes the causal horizon to preregistered three- and five-session
selloff exhaustion rather than retuning v3291.
