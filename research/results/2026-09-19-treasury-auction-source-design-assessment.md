# Treasury auction outcome design assessment (no preregistration)

Scope remains the frozen 2021–2023 event-cube sample of 527 symbols: **coverage-limited, not full market**. No auction training corpus or post-release stock outcomes were downloaded or ranked in this assessment.

The [TreasuryDirect historical auction-result archive](https://www.treasurydirect.gov/auctions/announcements-data-results/announcement-results-press-releases/) identifies official release-specific PDFs. The [TreasuryDirect securities API documentation](https://www.treasurydirect.gov/legal-information/developers/web-api-security/) points to the official securities service; a read-only January 2021 query returned 42 records with `pdfFilenameCompetitiveResults` and `xmlFilenameCompetitiveResults`. One exact official [auction XML](https://www.treasurydirect.gov/xml/R_20210126_3.xml) contains `AuctionDate=2021-01-26`, `ReleaseTime=13:01`, `BidToCoverRatio=2.34`, `HighYield=0.424`, and the corresponding result-PDF filename. Treasury's [historical timeline](https://www.treasurydirect.gov/research-center/timeline/auctions/) says the official release time is the recorded delivery time of the results XML to the Federal Reserve Information Technology server. These observations establish a promising point-in-time source, not an immutable complete 2021–2023 inventory. The historical dynamic index is currently inaccessible as a listing; the official API could supply a frozen inventory if a valid hypothesis passed design screening.

A plausible equity transmission is interest-rate sensitivity: use a strictly pre-publication stock beta to long Treasury ETF TLT, then rank auction-demand innovations. This proxy has poor sample coverage. In the frozen cube's bar-5 training rows, TLT has 65 distinct finite sessions in 2021, 129 in 2022, and 163 in 2023 (357 total). After excluding duplicate/nonfinite symbol-session keys, only from 2022-10-10 onward does any sample session have at least 150 non-TLT symbols with 60 earlier TLT-paired bar-5 returns. Across **all** 753 sample sessions, an optimistic upper bound gives 0 such sessions in 2021, 58 in 2022, and 250 in 2023. Auction-only dates and actual beta variance can only reduce those counts. IEF has just 29 bar-5 dates, AGG 9, BND 2; LQD/HYG have more observations but blend credit exposure and are not an unambiguous Treasury-duration proxy.

Thus the examined 60-paired-TLT duration mapping cannot support a three-year per-family training gate, even before any official auction return data. Do not lower the lookback merely to repair coverage or relabel credit beta as pure Treasury duration. This is a **design-stage rejection of this mapping**, not a 400-cell rejection of every possible Treasury-auction hypothesis; no auction line was preregistered and no return cells were run. A distinct source with a demonstrably causal, adequately covered stock map is the next step.
