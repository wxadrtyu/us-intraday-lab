# Census weekly business applications: point-in-time source screen

Scope: frozen 2021–2023 training sample of 527 symbols, **coverage-limited, not full market**. No Census training dataset, stock outcomes, or grid cells were loaded.

The [Census weekly BFS page](https://www.census.gov/econ/bfs/data/weekly.html) currently exposes national, regional and state CSV series rather than an indexed succession of dated weekly first-publication files. Census says the weekly estimates were released weekly until August 29, 2024, but its [historical release archive](https://www.census.gov/econ/bfs/data/historic.html) lists 12 dated **monthly** reports in each of 2021, 2022 and 2023, not 52 archived weekly files per year. Census [methodology](https://www.census.gov/econ/bfs/methodology.html) describes revisions to earlier observations, including annual updates to NAICS codes; thus the current weekly CSV cannot be treated as the first-published historical weekly values. The 36 monthly reports are below the already required 40 independent signal sessions for a retained cell, even before warm-up or missingness.

No complete, official weekly vintage inventory was established in the inspected official archive. Therefore do not preregister or download the current revised CSV for a point-in-time ranking exercise, or silently treat the 36 monthly PDFs as 156 weekly releases. This is a source-design rejection on current evidence, not a claim that no weekly vintage exists anywhere. Next assess another genuinely distinct official, date-specific source and an adequate causal stock exposure map.
