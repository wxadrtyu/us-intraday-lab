# U.S. Department of Labor weekly UI claims: pre-registration source assessment

This assesses a new labor-demand information source after the EIA WPSR line was frozen at its coverage gate. It is **not** a training outcome or a strategy candidate. The fixed research sample remains the 527 symbols in the 2021-2023 frozen event cube, a coverage-limited sample rather than the full market.

The [Department of Labor Office of Unemployment Insurance claims archive](https://oui.doleta.gov/unemploy/claims_arch.asp) offers historical weekly news releases through its `News Release`/year form. The official form's year responses for 2021, 2022, and 2023 each listed 52 release-specific PDF links; representative archive-linked PDFs for [2021](https://oui.doleta.gov/press/2021/010721.pdf), [2022](https://oui.doleta.gov/press/2022/010622.pdf), and [2023](https://oui.doleta.gov/press/2023/010523.pdf) returned HTTP 200 with PDF content type. The archive states the usual release time is Thursday 8:30 a.m. Eastern, with holiday exceptions. A [2020 pretraining release](https://oui.doleta.gov/press/2020/010220.pdf) explicitly carries its embargo date/time and first-page advance seasonally adjusted initial-claim and insured-unemployment figures. The official archive is a publication record; mere date-specific URLs do not prove immutable historical bytes. A research acquisition would preserve the three raw year-index responses, exact linked PDFs, retrieval metadata, SHA-256, missingness, and page-level extraction evidence; changed hashes or missing links must fail closed.

This source is economically distinct from the failed SEC issuer filings, FINRA short flow, Cboe volatility, Fed policy, CFTC positioning, USPTO patents, and EIA petroleum stocks: first-released unemployment claims describe labor-market separations and continuing unemployment, not those earlier signals. A possible broad cross-stock exposure proxy is the frozen sample's own prior bar-5 opening-return beta to SPY, which is in the 527-symbol sample. A read-only structural audit of training-period bar-5 presence (no UI-claims PDF or post-release outcome ranking) found 116 of 152 Friday proxy sessions with at least 150 non-SPY symbols having 60 SPY-paired observations strictly before Thursday. The actual release-calendar and beta-sign coverage remain untested; the 116 figure is not a feasibility pass.

Before downloading any 2021-2023 release PDF, preregister exact extraction, point-in-time availability, exposure cutoff, family predicates, missingness/coverage gates, fixed grid, 9/18 bp cost and one-bar delay tests. Do not use the mutable latest `data.pdf` or current revised claims time series as a historical substitute. No broker, Paper, pool or shutdown action is authorized.
