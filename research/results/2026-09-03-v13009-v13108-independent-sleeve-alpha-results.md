# v13009-v13108 independently activated triple-window alpha

The preregistered batch completed 100 versions and 1,000 parameter cells in
25.67 seconds. No candidate passed every pre-native-null gate; no native null
or Paper-pool change was allowed.

Independent sleeve activation materially weakened the structure. The best
candidate produced 57.56% annualized with 22.02% MDD and IR 1.28 under the
standard scenario; at 18bp it fell to 45.11% with 23.33% MDD. Historical
standard performance was negative and neighborhood pass share was zero.

The best z-score was 1.013 and the maximum anywhere in the batch was only
1.124, both far below the prior joint-gated triple-window leader's 3.275.
Independent activation therefore has no defensible local optimization path.
The next batch should retain the joint daily gate and test complementary factor
families across the three sleeves. The next unused version is v13109.
