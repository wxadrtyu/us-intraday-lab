# v349-v448 preregistered multifactor campaign

## Decision

The batch completed all 100 independently numbered versions: 50 causal state
enhancements of the admitted v45 structure and 50 independent factor/timing
return-source hypotheses. No version from v46 through v348 was rerun.

No candidate is admitted. None of the 300 frozen frontier records passed the
pre-factory-null gates, so no native factory null was eligible or run. The
global comparison family now covers 34,160 cells across the prior and current
campaigns.

## Validation boundary

- Factors and parameters were selected on 2022-2025 only.
- 2026 was attached after each three-record frontier froze and used only as a
  consumed diagnostic requiring total return above 5%.
- Every record includes 9bp, 18bp, an additional five-minute entry delay,
  five folds, start-date stress, parameter-neighborhood stress, and the
  independent 2018-2020 historical source.
- Every record was subjected to cumulative Bonferroni correction over all
  34,160 scanned cells.

The strongest unadjusted OOS result was v363 (`breadth_technology at bar17`):
52.76% annualized return during 2024-2025 and 31.83% consumed 2026 total return.
It is rejected, not promoted, because the full stress and global-comparison
contract did not pass.

enough to survive execution, stability, independent-history, and
multiple-comparison pressure simultaneously.
