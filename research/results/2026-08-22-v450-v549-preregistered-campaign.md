# v450-v549 preregistered multifactor campaign

## Decision

All 100 independently numbered versions completed: 50 causal-state
enhancements and 50 independent factor/timing return-source hypotheses. No
version from v46 through v449 was rerun.

No candidate is admitted. None of the 300 frozen frontier records passed all
pre-factory-null gates, so no native factory null was eligible or run. The
global comparison family conservatively covers 40,394 cells, including prior
campaigns, component-weight audits, meta-factor diagnostics, and this batch.

## Validation boundary

- Factor and parameter selection used 2022-2025 only.
- Consumed 2026 was attached only after each three-record frontier froze and
  was never used for ranking.
- Every record includes standard 9bp, 18bp, an additional five-minute entry
  delay, start-date stress, five chronological folds, parameter-neighborhood
  stress, and the independent 2018-2020 historical source.
- Cumulative Bonferroni pressure used all 40,394 counted cells.
